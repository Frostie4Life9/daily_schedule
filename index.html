    The two Shortcuts

    Open

    1. Protocol Timer receives a duration from this page and starts a real timer.
    New Shortcut, named exactly what you put in the Shortcut box above. One action: Start Timer. Set the unit to seconds and tap the number field, then pick the Shortcut Input variable. That is the whole thing. Test it by turning the toggle on and tapping any dial on the board.

    2. Protocol Reminders writes your alerts into Reminders each morning.
    Actions in order: Get Contents of URL pointed at your sync URL, with method GET and a header x-sync-key set to your sync key (both are on the Sync tab). Then Get Dictionary Value, key alerts. Then Repeat with Each. Inside the repeat: Get Dictionary Value key l from Repeat Item for the title, Get Dictionary Value key atPretty from Repeat Item, then Text reading Today at followed by that value, then Get Dates from Input, then Add New Reminder with the title and that date as the alert. End Repeat.
    Then in the Shortcuts app under Automation, add a Time of Day automation for whenever you wake, running that shortcut, with Run Immediately on and Notify When Run off.

    Alerts here are a published list, not a live connection. Change a time and the Shortcut picks it up the next morning or run it by hand to see it now.

    lessall clear

    One missed day never breaks the run. Two consecutive missed days do. Completing anything renews the grace, so the only way to lose a run is to actually stop for two days — not to have one late night.

    Everything in the app uses this, not just the streak — the board stops clearing itself out from under you, and a routine checked after midnight logs against the evening it belonged to. Set it later than you have ever been awake, earlier than you have ever woken up.
    One row per day: date, core steps done, core steps due. The heatmap lives in synced storage, so this is how you get it somewhere permanent — a spreadsheet, a note, anywhere that is not one Worker away from being gone.

    Monthly cost

    $0/mo · Open

    Grouped by what happens if you cut it. Every price and duration is editable — these are ballpark drugstore figures, not live prices. Put in what you actually pay and the math becomes yours. Duration is months per unit at daily use.

    There is no single correct price — the same 8oz Living Proof bottle sells for $14 to $40 depending on seller, and the cheap end is usually marketplace resellers where haircare gets diverted or counterfeited. Prices here are brand-direct anchors. Overwrite with what you actually paid.

    Duration is the number that matters more and the one nobody can look up. Hit Opened one today when you start a bottle and Ran out when it's done — after one cycle the estimate becomes measured, and the monthly figure becomes real.

    Ceilings are generic category defaults and they are the weakest numbers here — they can’t know that your hair type or skin puts you in a sub-category with a higher floor. When a research chat establishes what a category actually costs for you, overwrite the ceiling. That’s the number that makes the orange flags mean something.
